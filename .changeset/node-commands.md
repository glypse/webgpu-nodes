Copy the selected nodes (Ctrl/⌘+C) and paste them at the pointer (Ctrl/⌘+V), in any graph area, with the links between them; a repeat zone is always copied whole. Delete or Backspace deletes the selected nodes and links, Ctrl/⌘+Delete dissolves the selected nodes, linking what fed them to what they fed, and R resets the selected nodes' values, settings and picked types to their defaults.
