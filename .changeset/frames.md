Add frames, labelled boxes around nodes to organise a graph (Blender's frames): Ctrl/Cmd+J frames the selected nodes (inside the frame they share), or add an empty one from Add › Layout › Frame. A frame grows around what it holds. Drag a frame by its label to move it with its contents; drop nodes on a frame to put them in it, or off it to take them out; Alt+P takes the selected nodes out of their frame (into the one around it). Double-click a frame's label (or press N) to name it, write it a note shown under its label, and pick its colour. Frames nest; deleting a frame keeps what it held, while copying, duplicating or grouping a frame brings its contents along. Auto layout keeps each frame's nodes together, inside it.
