While playing, the timeline's frame rate shows the rate frames are drawn at against the rate playback aims for (e.g. 45/60 fps), the mean time per frame in milliseconds and, where the browser can time it, the GPU time per frame, each in red when it falls behind. In real time, the rate aimed for is the display's refresh rate rather than the project's. Hovering it gives the longest frame of the last second and the display's refresh rate.
