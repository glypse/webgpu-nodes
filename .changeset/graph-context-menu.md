Right-clicking the node graph opens a context menu instead of the add menu: on the canvas, Add… (opening the add menu there, as Shift+A still does) and Paste (greyed out with nothing copied); with nodes selected (a node right-clicked outside the selection becomes the selection), Cut, Copy, Paste and Duplicate, Delete and Dissolve, and Group selected for two nodes or more. Duplicate (also Shift+D) makes copies that follow the pointer until a click drops them; a right-click or Escape cancels. Right-clicking a text field keeps the browser's own menu.
