Menus, dialogs and dropdowns share one look: menu bar menus fade out when closing like the other menus, every submenu has the same outline and alignment, menus too tall for the window scroll instead of running off it, select lists get the same inner padding as menus, and popovers grow from their anchor. The node sheet's backdrop fades in like a dialog's, and the confirmation for removing unused groups has a single, lighter backdrop and starts with Cancel focused.
