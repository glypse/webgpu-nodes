Right-clicking the edge between two areas opens Blender's area edge options: Vertical split and Horizontal split (the split line then follows the pointer over any area until a click splits there; right-click or Escape cancels), Join right and Join left (or Join down and up, on an edge between areas one above the other), greyed out where the areas can't be joined, and Swap areas.
