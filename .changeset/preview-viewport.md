Zoom and pan the preview like the graph: the wheel (or a pinch) zooms around the pointer, dragging pans, a double click zooms in (out with Shift) and the corner button fits the canvas back into the preview. While the pointer is over the preview, + and − zoom, 0 fits and 1 shows the canvas pixel for pixel; the Display menu has the same actions. A bar under the preview shows the canvas resolution (opening the canvas settings) and the zoom, with a menu of zoom levels. Past 100%, pixels are drawn as sharp squares.
