A group can have several Group Input and Group Output nodes: add more from the add menu inside a group (Input › Group Input, Miscellaneous › Group Output), and place them where their values are used. Each shows all of the group's inputs or outputs; an output is linked on one Group Output node at a time (linking it on another moves it there), and is marked "(elsewhere)" on the others. Ctrl+H (Ctrl even on a Mac), or Hide unlinked sockets in the context menu, shows only a node's linked sockets, on any node; the eye button in its header shows them all again. Group boundary nodes can now be deleted, and copied within their group.
