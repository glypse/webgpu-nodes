Lay the editor out in areas, like Blender: every area has a header whose menu switches it between a node graph, a preview and a timeline. Drag an edge between areas to resize them (with Shift, every edge on that line moves), and drag an area's corner: into the area to split it, onto a neighbour near the edge you share to join them, elsewhere onto another area to dock it there (on one side, or in its middle to replace it), or with Ctrl to swap two areas. Right-clicking a header splits, maximizes (Ctrl+Space) or closes the area. Several node graphs can show different groups at once, each with its own breadcrumb and camera, and several previews each have their own zoom; menus and keys act on the area last used, or the one under the pointer. The layout is saved with the project.
