New Fractal Noise and OpenSimplex Noise textures, built on OpenSimplex2 (K.jpg's, public domain): 2D, 3D or 4D from the Vector's type, plain gradient noise for a scalar. Variant picks Smooth (OpenSimplex2S, the default) or Fast (OpenSimplex2); Orientation turns the lattice so the slices looked at avoid its axes, with the choices of the Vector's dimensions: Standard or Y Vertical in 2D; XY Plane (Z as time or height), XZ Plane (Y vertical) or Classic in 3D; and in 4D also XYZ Volume (W as time) and XY and ZW Planes (for noise(x, y, cos t, sin t)). A scalar shows neither Variant nor Orientation. Fractal Noise sums octaves as Blender's Noise Texture does, in its five types (fBm, Multifractal, Hybrid Multifractal, Heterogeneous Terrain, Ridged Multifractal), with Offset and Gain on the types that use them (each its own, defaulting to where it looks right: Ridged at Offset 1, Gain 2), Distortion and a Color output; each octave is moved at random so their lattices don't line up.
