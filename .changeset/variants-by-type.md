A group's enum variants can be offered only for some types of one of its type variables (in the group settings, under each variant): group nodes offer the variants their types have, and one holding a variant their types don't have picks the first one offered. Fractal Noise, OpenSimplex Noise, Noise Distortion and OpenSimplex Orientation have a single Orientation input whose options follow Vector's dimensions, keeping XY Plane, XZ Plane or Classic when switching between 3D and 4D.
