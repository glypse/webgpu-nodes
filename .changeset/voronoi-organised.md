Voronoi Texture and Fractal Voronoi are organised for reading and editing: their nodes sit in labelled frames with notes (Lattice, F1 and F2, Smooth F1, Edges, Radius; Reach, Octaves, First octave), values used far from where they're computed travel through named reroutes, and each frame has Group Input and Output nodes of its own. Voronoi Texture's four searches are helper groups of their own, found under Texture › Helpers › Voronoi: Voronoi Nearest Points, Voronoi Edges, Voronoi Radius and Voronoi Smooth F1. They compute exactly what they did.
