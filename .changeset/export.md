Export what the preview draws, from the File menu or the button beside the zoom: the frame shown as a PNG, at the canvas resolution and in the project's colour space, or a video (MP4 or WebM, at medium, high or very high quality). A video is rendered frame by frame from a start time, for a duration and at a frame rate (the project's by default), so it never drops frames whatever the playback, with a progress bar and cancel; it's encoded in sRGB.
